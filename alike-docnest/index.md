# aLike DocNest

**Offline. Private. Family Ready.**

A private, secure digital home for your family's important documents — no account, no cloud required.
Add family members, scan or import documents, find them fast, and get reminded before anything
expires. Everything is stored on your device and encrypted at rest, with optional encrypted backup to
your own Google Drive.
