# Changelog — aLike DocNest

## 1.0.0 — first public release

aLike DocNest's first public release — a private, offline-first home for your family's documents.

- **Family members** — add profiles (name, relationship, blood group, photo, emergency contact); your
  own "Self" profile is protected.
- **Documents** — scan with the camera (on-device), import files, and automatic on-device text
  recognition (OCR) that pre-fills details; open documents in a full-screen, multi-page viewer with
  selectable live text; organize by category and member.
- **Find & remind** — fast search across your documents, including natural-language queries; renewal
  reminders before documents expire, with a per-document reminder frequency.
- **Voice search** — search by speaking, processed entirely on your device (English, Hindi, Marathi).
- **Privacy & security** — works offline, no account; data encrypted at rest; optional PIN / biometric
  app lock.
- **Backup** — optional encrypted backup and restore to your own Google Drive.
- **Languages** — available in English, हिन्दी (Hindi), and मराठी (Marathi).
