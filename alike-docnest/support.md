# Support — aLike DocNest

Need help with aLike DocNest? We're happy to help.

**Email:** **eightelephantsgroup@gmail.com**

When reporting a problem, please include:

- your device model and Android version,
- the app version (Settings → About),
- and a short description of what happened (and steps to reproduce, if you can).

## Common questions

- **Where is my data?** On your device, encrypted at rest. Nothing is sent to us. See the
  [Privacy Policy](privacy.html).
- **How do I back up?** Settings → Backup → connect Google Drive. Backups are encrypted and stored in
  your own Drive's private app folder.
- **I forgot my PIN.** Restore from a backup on a fresh install to regain access.
