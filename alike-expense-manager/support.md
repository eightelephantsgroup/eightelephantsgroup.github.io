# aLike Expense Manager — Support

Need help? Try the FAQ below, or email **eightelephantsgroup@gmail.com** — please include "aLike" in the subject line.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where is my data stored?

On your phone. Specifically, in a local database file inside the app's private storage. We never see it. Read the [privacy policy](/alike-expense-manager/privacy.html) for the full picture.

### How do I back up my data?

Open **Settings → Backup & restore**. You can pick:

- **Local backup** — saves a file to a folder you choose. You can copy this anywhere.
- **Google Drive backup** — saves to your own Drive's "AppData" folder, a private space only this app can read.

We strongly recommend enabling one of these.

### I lost my phone / I'm moving to a new phone. How do I restore?

If you had Google Drive backup turned on:

1. Install aLike on the new phone.
2. Open **Settings → Backup & restore → Link Google Drive**.
3. Sign in with the same Google account you used before.
4. Tap **Restore** and pick the most recent backup.

If you only had a local backup file, copy it to the new phone and use **Settings → Backup & restore → Restore from file**.

### Does the app work offline?

Yes — every screen works without a network connection. The only network features are optional and explicitly turned on by you: Google Drive backup, and downloading a larger voice-recognition model.

### How does voice entry work?

You hold the microphone button, speak, and release. The app uses an on-device Whisper model to transcribe your voice into a transaction. Audio is **never** uploaded; it is processed and discarded on your phone.

### How do I get better voice accuracy, especially for numbers?

Open **Settings → Voice input** and download a larger model. Bigger models recognize numbers and accented speech more accurately. The download is opt-in and one-time.

### What is included in Pro?

Pro unlocks additional accounts beyond the free tier, additional budgets, additional recurring rules, and advanced reports. See the in-app **Settings → Get Pro** for the current list.

### How do I cancel / refund Pro?

Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. For refunds, use Google Play's refund flow (open the Play Store app → menu → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history). If you have trouble, email us.

### I found a bug.

Please email **eightelephantsgroup@gmail.com** with a description of what happened and, if possible, the steps to reproduce. Screenshots help a lot.

### Can I request a feature?

Yes — email is the best way. We read every message even if we cannot reply individually.
